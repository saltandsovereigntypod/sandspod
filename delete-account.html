Delete your account

You can delete your Salt & Sovereignty account, and everything kept with it, at any time.

In the app

  1. Open Salt & Sovereignty on your iPhone, iPad or Android phone and sign in.
  2. Go to More → Account → Delete account.
  3. If you like, download a backup first. Then confirm, and your account is deleted straight away.

Without the app

Email ashley.p@saltandsovereignty.com from the address you signed up with (or tell us that address, and whether you used Google or Apple to sign in) and ask for your account to be deleted. We'll confirm and delete it within 30 days, usually much sooner.

Deleting some of your data, but keeping your account

You can delete any altar, grimoire page or book, ritual plan, journal entry, Living Library note, apothecary working or picture on its own, in the app or on the website, and all of your Cycle & Moon data at once (Practice → Rituals → Cycle & Moon → Settings and privacy → Delete all my Cycle & Moon data). Each is removed from your account and stops syncing straight away. To have something else removed (for example, a community submission or your profile name), email ashley.p@saltandsovereignty.com and say what you'd like deleted; we'll do it within 30 days.

What's deleted with your account

  • Your account and sign-in details.
  • Your settings, altars, grimoire, rituals, journal, Living Library notes, apothecary, and the pictures you added.
  • Your private drafts and any community submissions that weren't published.
  • Your Cycle & Moon notes (kept with your account only in encrypted form) and any “in moon time” status you shared.
  • Your coven profile, memberships, signatures, applications and petitions, every message you wrote in a coven's chat or in your one-to-one and group conversations, your people blocked, and the phones signed up for your notifications.

What's kept

Pages and Field Notes already published in the Community Grimoire stay, but only in anonymized form, with your name and account removed (see the Submission Terms). A coven you founded stays for its members, without you as its founder, and pages you published in a coven's book stay there without your name. Reports you sent to the moderators, and reports about you with a copy of what was reported, are kept without your account so covens stay safe. Nothing else is kept. Guest data that never left your device is removed when you delete the app or clear your browser.
