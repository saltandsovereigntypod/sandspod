Covens

Coven community guidelines

A coven is a circle of trust. These guidelines keep every Salt & Sovereignty coven a safe place to practise.

Covens are for adults

You must be 18 or older to join, apply to or found a coven. If you think someone in a coven is under 18, please report them. We remove under-18 accounts from covens.

Joining and invitations

Each coven chooses how people join: open to anyone, by application, or only by invitation. An invitation, sent to you by name or shared as an invite link, lets you join without applying, even a coven that isn’t listed. Either way, you confirm you are 18 or older and sign the coven’s agreements before you join, and you may decline any invitation. Only invite people you believe will keep these guidelines, and never pressure anyone to accept.

Your freedom comes first

  • You may leave any coven at any time, without explaining why.
  • No one may pressure you for money, gifts, secrets, private photographs, sexual contact, your address or your obedience. A coven may ask members to help with shared costs, but never as a condition of belonging or of rising in position.
  • No one may tell you to cut off family or friends, stop medical or mental-health care, or harm yourself as part of your practice.
  • Initiation and advancement must never involve humiliation, danger, nudity or anything you don’t freely consent to.

Treat each other with respect

  • No harassment, bullying, threats or curses aimed at members.
  • No hate or discrimination based on race, ethnicity, religion or path, gender, sexuality, disability, age or body.
  • No sexual content or advances in covens.
  • Respect closed and living traditions; don’t present practices you weren’t given as yours to teach.
  • No spam, scams, or selling in the gathering unless the coven’s rules allow it.

Privacy

  • What members share in a coven stays in the coven. Don’t screenshot or repost it elsewhere without permission.
  • Never share someone’s real name, address or other personal details (“doxxing”).
  • Choose a coven name you’re comfortable with; it doesn’t have to be your legal name.
  • To keep covens safe, the Salt & Sovereignty moderators can read every coven’s pages, gathering and altar, and the reports people send.

Leading a coven

Founders and the members they give powers to are trusted with others’ safety. Write your creed, rules, requirements and agreements honestly, apply them fairly, and don’t use your position to pressure anyone. Coven rules can add to these guidelines but can’t override them.

Each coven’s leaders keep track of how its members take part: which rites they come to (someone counts as there when they open a rite while it’s under way), how their initiation, training and mentoring are going, and counts of what else they do. A coven may ask members who no longer take part to leave. Leaders never see what you write privately or say in your own conversations, and you can see your own record in the coven under My standing → Your record.

A coven’s head, and those they choose, can also see the paths in the coven: what share of its members practise each way, keep each path and honour each deity, as patrons or otherwise. It is only ever a share, never who keeps what. Anything fewer than three members share is gathered under “Other”, and nothing shows until five have shared. If you’d rather not be counted, turn off “Count me in my covens’ paths overview” in Settings → Covens & privacy.

Positions and advancement

A coven’s leaders may open a position for applications, limit how many may hold it, and say what it asks: time in the coven or in a position, rites attended, a program completed, a page signed, a mentor’s approval, or something in their own words. Write these honestly and apply them to everyone alike. What the coven can check from its own record is checked for you, and you can see where you stand before you apply; the rest is for the reviewers to confirm.

  • Asking to advance is always your choice. No one may pressure you to apply, or to stay in a position you no longer want.
  • A requirement may never ask for money, gifts, secrets, private photographs, sexual contact, your address or your obedience, nor anything in “Your freedom comes first” above.
  • When a position is full or has closed, requests already made are still answered, kindly and in good time.
  • Leaders choose who hears that a position is open. Each person can turn these notifications off, and no one who has blocked a leader is told.

Reporting and blocking

Every message, member, application and coven has a Report button. Reports go to the Salt & Sovereignty moderators, never to the coven’s leaders, and the person you report isn’t told who reported them. You can also block someone so you no longer see their messages.

Moderators may remove messages, pause members, pause or archive a coven, or ban an account from all covens.

If you or someone else is in danger

Covens are not a crisis service. If anyone is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency number. Support lines for many situations are listed in the Sanctuary.

These guidelines sit alongside the privacy policy and the submission terms.
