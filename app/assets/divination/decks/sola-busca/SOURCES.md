# Sources: Sola Busca (`sola-busca`)

The 78 cards of the Sola Busca tarot: copper engravings made in northern
Italy (Ferrara or Venice) about 1491, attributed to Nicola di Maestro Antonio
d'Ancona, and coloured by hand. The only complete coloured deck belonged to
the Sola Busca family of Milan and is now in the Pinacoteca di Brera, Milan.
It is the oldest complete 78-card deck, and Pamela Colman Smith drew several
of her minor cards from it.

**Status.** The engravings are more than five hundred years old and in the
public domain everywhere. The files are flat colour reproductions of the
cards, uploaded to Wikimedia Commons in September 2026 as
"Sola Busca tarot card 00.jpg" … "77.jpg" (the uploader's source:
<https://waitesmith.org/index.php/decks/the-sola-busca-deck/>) and tagged
`{{PD-Art|PD-old-100-expired}}`: faithful reproductions of two-dimensional
public-domain works, which carry no new copyright (Commons' PD-Art policy;
EU Directive 2019/790, article 14). They are reproductions of the old cards,
age and all, not a modern redrawing or recolouring.

Not used: "7 of Discs from Sola-Busca Tarot.jpg" (CC BY-SA 3.0, a single
card), and the British Museum's uncoloured impressions (single cards, and the
museum's own licence is CC BY-NC-SA).

**Downloaded** in October 2026 as Commons' 330 px-wide thumbnails of the
original 474 × 877 files (Wikimedia's rate limits made the full-size files
impractical), directly from `upload.wikimedia.org`. Every card was checked by
eye against its number and suit before it was kept.

**Made into** `assets/divination/decks/sola-busca/<card id>.webp` for the
website: each scan scaled to 600 px tall and set on a 350 × 600 card, its
sides filled with the scan's own edge colour. The app's sheets here
(`sheet-1.webp` … `sheet-6.webp`) are built from those by
`app/scripts/build-divination-assets.py`.

**Mapping.** Card ids are the app's (`app/src/lib/divination/tarot.ts`). The
trumps are named figures numbered 0 to XXI and map by number to the standard
majors, keeping their own names as captions (0 Mato is `00-the-fool`, VIII
Nerone `08-strength`, XXI Nabuchodenasor `21-the-world`, and so on). The
Commons files run 00–21 trumps, 22–35 Cups, 36–49 Coins, 50–63 Batons and
64–77 Swords, each Ace to King; Coins are the app's Pentacles and Batons its
Wands, and the four court cards (fante, cavallo, regina, re) its Page, Knight,
Queen and King. The meanings shown are the app's own, with the deck's own
title beside them (`deckCardTitle` in `app/src/lib/divination/decks.ts`).

**Nudity.** Several cards show nude or partly clothed figures and putti, as
Renaissance engravings do: among them 0 Mato; the Four, Seven, Eight and
Nine of Cups; the Ace, Three, Four and Seven of Coins; the Ace, Two and Nine
of Batons; and the Two, Six, Seven, Eight and Nine of Swords.
