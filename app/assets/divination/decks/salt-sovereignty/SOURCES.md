# Sources: Salt & Sovereignty Tarot (`salt-sovereignty`)

An original 78-card deck illustrated for Salt & Sovereignty in 2026, made
to the style guide in `docs/tarot/style-guide.md`: one mossy forest, no
people, with the suits Wands, Cauldrons (Cups), Athames (Swords) and
Pentacles, and the courts Seeker, Wanderer, Witch and Elder (Page, Knight,
Queen, King). The frame and each card's title are part of the picture.

**Status.** Our own work, not public domain; not for reuse outside the app
and website without permission.

The finished cards (600 × 1030 PNG) were resized to 350 × 600 and saved as
WebP in `../../../../../assets/divination/decks/salt-sovereignty/`, named by
the card ids in `app/src/lib/divination/tarot.ts`; the app's six sheets are
made from those by `app/scripts/build-divination-assets.py`. It reads with
the Rider–Waite–Smith meanings (A. E. Waite's, in `tarot.ts`).
