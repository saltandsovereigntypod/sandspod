# Sources: Tarot de Marseille (`marseille`)

The 78 cards of the Tarot de Marseille in Nicolas Conver's pattern (his deck
of 1760, "N. Conver 1760"), in the printing published by A. Camoin, Marseille,
between about 1890 and 1900: woodcut, coloured by stencil. The deck is in the
Bibliothèque nationale de France, département des Estampes et de la
photographie, BOITE FOL-KH-383 (11, 263), from the collection of Paul
Marteau; Gallica record `ark:/12148/btv1b10539497f`
(<https://gallica.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/btv1b10539497f>).

**Status.** The artwork is public domain: Conver's design is from 1760 and
the Camoin printing from the 1890s. The scans are the BnF's own flat
reproductions of the printed cards (no restoration or recolouring), uploaded
to Wikimedia Commons from Gallica by Gzen92Bot and tagged there
`{{PD-France}}`, `{{PD-old-auto-expired|1833}}` and `{{PD-US-expired}}`.
Faithful reproductions of two-dimensional public-domain works carry no new
copyright in the EU (Directive 2019/790, article 14) or the US, and Commons
treats them as public domain (PD-Art).

Not used: the CBD Tarot de Marseille, and the Flornoy and Reynaud
restorations (modern, under their makers' licences); the 2020 Reality
Publishing reprint of Conver on Commons (CC BY-SA, and incomplete); and the
BnF's Conver printing of about 1809–1833 (`btv1b10520316w`), which has only 66
of the 78 cards.

**Downloaded** in October 2026 as Commons' 330 px-wide thumbnails of the
original files (Wikimedia's rate limits made the full-size files
impractical), directly from `upload.wikimedia.org`. The 156 files are each
card's face and back in turn; only the faces (the odd numbers) are used.
Every card was checked by eye against its name before it was kept.

**Made into** `assets/divination/decks/marseille/<card id>.webp` for the
website: each scan scaled to 600 px tall and set on a 350 × 600 card, its
sides filled with the scan's own edge colour. The app's sheets here
(`sheet-1.webp` … `sheet-6.webp`) are built from those by
`app/scripts/build-divination-assets.py`.

**Mapping.** Card ids are the app's (`app/src/lib/divination/tarot.ts`).
Conver numbers Justice VIII and Strength XI, so his VIII La Justice is the
app's `11-justice` and his XI La Force its `08-strength`; the other trumps
map by number, and Le Mat is `00-the-fool`. Suits: Bâtons → Wands, Coupes →
Cups, Épées → Swords, Deniers → Pentacles; Valet, Cavalier, Reyne, Roy →
Page, Knight, Queen, King. The meanings shown are the app's own, with the
deck's own title beside them (`deckCardTitle` in
`app/src/lib/divination/decks.ts`).

**Nudity.** Le Diable (XV), L'Étoile (XVII), Le Soleil (XVIIII, two children
in loincloths), Le Jugement (XX), Le Monde (XXI) and the nameless XIII show
nude or near-nude figures, as the woodcut tradition draws them.
